03 : About me

What I do

I'm a Full Stack Developer based in Copenhagen, building web applications with Vue 3, Node.js and Python. At AffiliateMotion I work on dashboard UI/UX and integrations with affiliate networks such as AWIN, CJ, Impact and Webgains. That means turning messy third-party data into clean, reliable tools that people actually use.

Beyond the day job

Outside work I built a multi-network affiliate tool: a Vue frontend on Vercel, Playwright scrapers on Railway and a shared Supabase Postgres database. I also explore AI/ML, from Deep Q-Learning agents to fine-tuning LLaMA 2 with LoRA/PEFT.

Background & what I'm looking for

I hold a Professional Diploma in Computer Science and a CCNA Cyber Security certification. Years of working in busy restaurants taught me to stay calm under pressure,communicate clearly and work well in a team. I'm looking for a junior-to-mid full stack or backend role in Copenhagen where I can keep growing and ship things that matter.
